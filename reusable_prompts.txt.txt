Here is a **reusable prompt toolkit** designed for undergraduate students reading AI research papers. You can copy and paste any of these prompts directly into an AI assistant whenever you need help analyzing academic papers.

---

### **Prompt 1: Summarize a Research Paper**

```text
Please provide a clear, beginner-friendly summary of the following research paper:

[PASTE PAPER TEXT HERE]

Follow these rules strictly:
1. Give a 3 to 4 sentence overview of what the paper is about in simple English.
2. Highlight 3 key points or takeaways using short bullet points.
3. Use ONLY information directly stated in the provided paper text. Do NOT invent facts, statistics, quotes, or citations.
4. If a key topic or detail is missing or unclear in the paper, explicitly write: "Not stated in the provided source."
5. Include section names, paragraph numbers, or direct quotes as evidence for each major claim.
```

---

### **Prompt 2: Extract Aim, Method, Findings, and Limitations**

```text
Analyze the following research paper and extract its core structural components:

[PASTE PAPER TEXT HERE]

Please break down your response into these exact sections:
1. Paper Title and Authors
2. Research Aim (What did the researchers want to find out?)
3. Research Method (How did they conduct the study, including sample size and tools used?)
4. Main Findings (What were their main discoveries or statistics?)
5. Limitations (What could the study not establish or what constraints did they face?)

Rules to follow:
- Base every single statement strictly on the text provided above.
- Do NOT guess, extrapolate, or invent details.
- If any of these sections are missing or not clearly stated in the text, write: "Not stated in the available source."
- Provide exact page numbers, section headers, or short quotes from the paper to back up each item.
- Use simple, plain English that an undergraduate student can easily understand.
```

---

### **Prompt 3: Compare Two or More Research Papers**

```text
Compare the following research papers:

--- PAPER 1 ---
[PASTE PAPER 1 TEXT HERE]

--- PAPER 2 ---
[PASTE PAPER 2 TEXT HERE]

--- PAPER 3 (Optional) ---
[PASTE PAPER 3 TEXT HERE OR REMOVE THIS SECTION]

Please provide:
1. A Literature Review Table comparing the papers across these columns: Paper Title, Core Focus/Aim, Methodology, Main Findings, and Key Limitations.
2. Three major SIMILARITIES between the papers.
3. Three major DIFFERENCES between the papers.

Strict rules:
- Base all comparisons ONLY on the provided paper texts. Do NOT bring in outside knowledge or invent comparisons.
- Clearly attribute findings to the specific paper title/author.
- If a paper does not mention a specific detail compared in the table, write "Not stated in source."
- Include source citations or direct quotes next to factual claims.
- Write in beginner-friendly English.
```

---

### **Prompt 4: Explain Technical Terms in Simple English**

```text
Help me understand the technical jargon in the following research paper:

[PASTE PAPER TEXT HERE]

Identify 5 to 10 difficult technical terms, acronyms, or methods used in this paper. For each term, provide:
1. Technical Term (exact term used in the text)
2. Simple Definition (explain it in plain English suitable for an undergraduate beginner)
3. Educational or Real-World Example (a short, concrete example showing how it applies)
4. Paper Context (quote the sentence from the paper where this term appears and note its section/page reference)

Rules:
- Select terms that actually appear in the text above. Do NOT invent or add terms not found in the paper.
- If the paper's usage or definition of a term is vague or incomplete, state: "The text does not fully explain this term."
- Keep explanations simple, clear, and free of unnecessary jargon.
```

### **Prompt 5: Fact-Check an AI Summary Against the Original Paper**

```text
I want you to act as an error-checker. Compare the AI-generated summary below against the original research paper source text.

--- ORIGINAL RESEARCH PAPER ---
[PASTE ORIGINAL PAPER TEXT HERE]

--- AI SUMMARY TO CHECK ---
[PASTE AI-GENERATED SUMMARY HERE]

Create an Error-Checking Table with these 5 columns:
1. Claim Made in Summary
2. Evidence in Original Paper
3. Verification Status (Mark as: Verified, Incorrect, or Unclear)
4. Explanation
5. Correction Needed (If Incorrect)

Rules:
- Verify every single statistic, sample size, claim, author name, and conclusion.
- Do NOT invent errors if the summary is accurate. If no errors are found, say so honestly.
- Mark a claim as "Unclear" if the original text does not provide enough information to confirm or disprove it.
- Cite specific sections, page numbers, or exact quotes from the original paper for every check.
- Use plain, simple English.
```
